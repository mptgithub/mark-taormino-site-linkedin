About

Leading educators through the AI frontier

Dr. Mark Taormino is a professor and industry thought leader in Instructional Technology, recognized as an education trailblazer in integrating artificial intelligence across higher education. He holds a Doctorate in Instructional Technology and Online Education, and is a recognized expert in distance education and digital literacy for college students. His forward-thinking initiatives for faculty and students have been featured in eCampus News for driving institutional innovation.

He brings a vital, unique perspective to today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, helping educators move beyond tech adoption to unlock truly creative and effective teaching methodologies. In his keynote speeches, he offers actionable insights on navigating the AI frontier and empowering instructors to lead the next generation of classroom and online learning.

"Helping educators move beyond tech adoption to unlock truly creative, effective teaching."
